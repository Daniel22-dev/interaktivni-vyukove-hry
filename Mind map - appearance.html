This lab tests meaning, collocation, lexical grammar, dictionary skills, register and active production. Most tasks require doing something with the language — matching, building, typing, correcting, sorting or explaining.

What this result means

The score is based on active lexical knowledge: definitions, collocations, patterns, dictionary information, sentence correction and register. The final explanation task is deliberately self-assessed because several good definitions are possible.

Differentiation
Weaker students

Work in pairs. Allow a paper/online dictionary after Station 2. Students must still explain why they chose an answer in English.

Stronger students

No dictionary until Station 4. After each station, require one original sentence using a target item naturally.

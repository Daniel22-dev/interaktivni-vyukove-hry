Task A / Main ideas

Main ideas and arguments

Choose the one best answer (A-D) for each question. Listen for meaning and the speaker's reasoning, not isolated words.

6 points
Task B / Details

True / False / Not Stated

Decide whether each statement is supported by the recording (TRUE), contradicted by it (FALSE), or not established in the recording (NOT STATED). Do not use outside knowledge.

6 points
Task C / Advice and reasons

Why does the speaker recommend it?

Match each piece of advice (C1-C5) with the reason the speaker gives for it (A-G). Each letter can be used once. Two reasons are extra. Choose the reason given in the recording, not simply one that sounds sensible.

5 points
